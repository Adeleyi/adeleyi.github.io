Esther Adenuga |

Computational Linguist | NLP Research Fellow

Hello👋🏽 My name is Esther Adenuga. I’m an NLP Research Fellow at The African Research Collective, where I work on projects that advance technology for African languages.

I’m interested in how computers understand meaning and context in natural language, and how this knowledge can be applied to create linguistically informed and safe tools for low-resource and multilingual settings.

Outside of research, I work as an ESL tutor, run a reading community for teenagers, take photos of nature, get lost in thriller novevls, and, once in a while, cheer (or suffer🙂) as a loyal Chelsea fan.
